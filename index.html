Service and academic integrity policies

Our services are scoped and quoted before work begins. We provide coaching, editing, data interpretation support, approved consulting and guided technical development. Students remain responsible for their assessed work. We do not fabricate research data or citations, impersonate students or offer concealed ghostwriting. Free Turnitin similarity checks are available only for qualifying packages when authorised licence access exists; AI-writing indicators, where available, are not definitive authorship judgments. Milestone payments, cancellations, revision limits and technical ownership are agreed per engagement. We do not guarantee grades or institutional approval. Formal privacy and contractual documents should be finalised before collecting or storing client data.
